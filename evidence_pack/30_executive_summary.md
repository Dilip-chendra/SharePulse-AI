# 30. Executive Summary & Evidence Pack Synthesis
**Synchrony Analytics Hackathon 2026 Submission**
**Evaluation Subject**: MetroMart Inc. & HSIC Bank Co-Branded Credit Card Portfolio
**Data Basis**: 444,118 Verified Transactions | 45,000 Cardholders | FY25 - FY26

---

## 1. Top 5 Verified Findings (Empirically Proven)

1. **Share-of-Wallet Contraction (-9.43 pp drop)**:
   - HSIC captured **28.91%** of active cardholder spend in FY25, which declined sharply to **19.48%** in FY26.
   - Total customer spend at MetroMart grew +19.39%, proving customers are not churning from the retailer, but actively substituting payment methods away from HSIC.
   - *Evidence Type*: **OBSERVED** (100% Transaction Ledger Validation).

2. **MetroMart Wallet is the Dominant Payment Rail**:
   - Closed-loop MetroMart Wallet accounts for **35.68%** of active cardholder spend in FY26 (+₹24.3M net spend YoY).
   - Cash/UPI expanded +4.51 pp (reaching 23.40% share in FY26), dominating frequent low-ticket everyday baskets.
   - *Evidence Type*: **OBSERVED**.

3. **Prime Cashback Forfeiture Paradox (₹5,516,360.84 Unclaimed Rewards)**:
   - 19,423 Prime cardholders forfeited **₹5,516,360.84** in cashback by using non-HSIC rails on qualifying spend (where they were eligible for 5% Grocery and 3% Electronics rewards).
   - *Evidence Type*: **OBSERVED**.

4. **Big-Ticket Durables Inversion Point (> ₹5,000)**:
   - In Large Appliances and Electronics transactions exceeding ₹5,000, HSIC SoW drops to **11.20%** (versus 24.80% in low-ticket categories) as shoppers switch to competing credit cards offering zero-cost POS financing.
   - *Evidence Type*: **OBSERVED**.

5. **Silent Defectors Representing Significant Annualized Revenue at Risk**:
   - Machine learning risk modeling (Hist Gradient Boosting) reveals a predictable **5-month slow-fade glide path**, providing a 90-day lead-time intervention window.
   - *Evidence Type*: **MODEL-DERIVED**.

## 3. Top 3 Strategic Recommendations & Economics

1. **Strategy 1: 1-Click Default Card Binding & Prime Statement Transparency**:
   - Recaptures forfeited Prime cashback with zero discount dilution. Expected Net Contribution: **₹5.2M**.
2. **Strategy 2: 0% Merchant-Subsidized POS Financing on Durables (> ₹5,000)**:
   - Closes the big-ticket financing gap against competitor bank credit cards. Expected Net Contribution: **₹3.8M**.
3. **Strategy 3: Closed-Loop A/B Experimentation Engine**:
   - Validates predictive NBA offers with strict control holdouts and automated ROI attribution.

---

## 4. Top 3 Things the Data Cannot Prove (Analytical Disclaimers)

1. **Refund Turnaround Time**: Return volume is observed (4.8% by value), but exact refund credit settlement duration is unrecorded.
2. **Off-Us Spend Outside MetroMart**: Total customer credit line usage outside MetroMart is unobservable.
3. **Causal Campaign Lift**: Historical data is purely observational; true causal uplift must be verified via the proposed A/B pilot trial designs.
