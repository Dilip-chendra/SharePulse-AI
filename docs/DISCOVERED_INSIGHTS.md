## 4-Tier Knowledge Taxonomy
- **OBSERVED:** Deterministic historical fact calculated directly from raw transaction/customer records.
- **MODEL_DERIVED:** Inference or prediction generated by machine learning models, clustering algorithms, or dimensionality reduction.
- **HYPOTHESIS:** Plausible behavioral, commercial, or UI friction mechanism explaining observed empirical patterns.
- **PROPOSED:** Actionable intervention, next best action playbook, or counterfactual policy recommendation.

## Top Discovered Findings Beyond Initial Case Requirements

### 1. Prime Member Benefit Paradox [OBSERVED]
- **Empirical Evidence:** Prime members holding the HSIC card spent **₹331,207,664.88** via Non-HSIC payment methods, forfeiting **₹5,516,360.84** in direct cashback (₹2.23M in Electronics @ 3%, ₹898k in Grocery @ 5%).
- **Statistical Evidence:** $N = 135,934$ Non-HSIC Prime transactions, $p < 0.0001$.
- **Hypothesis [HYPOTHESIS]:** Checkout UI auto-selects MetroMart Wallet / UPI without showing real-time foregone cashback.
- **Proposed Action [PROPOSED]:** Implement "Missed Savings Checkout Notification" + 1-click default card setting.
- **Economic Potential:** ₹35,000,000 addressable spend recovery.

### 2. High-Ticket Basket Share Inversion [OBSERVED]
- **Empirical Evidence:** Transactions $\ge$ ₹5,000 represent **51.54%** of total sales volume (₹377.39M). In this high-margin basket tier, MetroMart Wallet captures **40.96%** share vs HSIC's **25.24%**.
- **Statistical Evidence:** Two-sample proportion $z$-test: $z = 47.82, p < 0.0001$, Cohen's $h = 0.33$.
- **Hypothesis [HYPOTHESIS]:** Wallet reload incentives and instant cashbacks dominate sales events, cannibalizing credit card financing.
- **Proposed Action [PROPOSED]:** Deploy instant 2% statement rebate or 3-month 0% EMI financing for transactions $\ge$ ₹5,000.
- **Economic Potential:** ₹42,500,000 incremental spend.

### 3. Silent Defection Spend Displacement [MODEL_DERIVED]
- **Empirical Evidence:** **9,049 active cardholders** maintained continuous shopping at MetroMart while displacing **₹58,329,429.66** in spend away from HSIC to Wallet & UPI (mean drop of ₹6,446 per customer).
- **Statistical Evidence:** Paired longitudinal $t$-test: $t = 118.4, p < 0.0001$, Cohen's $d = 1.24$.
- **Hypothesis [HYPOTHESIS]:** Cardholders experience zero friction in shifting payments to UPI/Wallet as no explicit card cancellation is needed.
- **Proposed Action [PROPOSED]:** Deploy automated 60-day moving window SoW decline triggers offering ₹250 statement credits.
- **Economic Potential:** ₹28,000,000 recovered spend.

### 4. New Cohort Onboarding Deterioration [OBSERVED]
- **Empirical Evidence:** Older cohorts (2021-2024) opened with baseline SoWs of 28.38% - 29.67%. In contrast, newly onboarded 2026 cardholders opened at an unprecedented baseline of only **16.27% SoW** (-43% relative collapse).
- **Statistical Evidence:** Welch's two-sample $t$-test: $t = -14.62, p < 0.0001$, Cohen's $d = -0.58$.
- **Hypothesis [HYPOTHESIS]:** Digital card issuance lacks instant in-app tokenization and payment default setting at activation.
- **Proposed Action [PROPOSED]:** Launch First 90 Days Milestone Cashback Multiplier (₹100 in Month 1, ₹200 in Month 2, ₹300 in Month 3).
- **Economic Potential:** ₹18,500,000 cohort lifetime value.

### 5. Competitor Bank Infiltration in Large Durables [OBSERVED]
- **Empirical Evidence:** Competing Bank Credit Cards capture their highest portfolio shares in Large Appliances (10.56%, ₹12.0M) and Furniture (10.32%, ₹10.3M).
- **Statistical Evidence:** $\chi^2 = 29.41, p = 0.0006$, Cramer's $V = 0.08$.
- **Hypothesis [HYPOTHESIS]:** High card APR (31.5% portfolio average) causes durables buyers to seek competitor subsidized OEM EMI offers.
- **Proposed Action [PROPOSED]:** Form direct manufacturer EMI co-op partnerships with white-goods brands.
- **Economic Potential:** ₹14,000,000 incremental spend.

### 6. Return Rate Neutrality Across Payment Channels [OBSERVED]
- **Empirical Evidence:** Product return rates are virtually identical across HSIC (11.18%), Wallet (11.20%), UPI (11.23%), Debit (11.30%), and Other CC (11.07%).
- **Statistical Evidence:** Chi-square test of independence: $\chi^2 = 0.7397 (\text{df}=4), p = 0.9464$. Fail to reject null hypothesis.
- **Hypothesis [HYPOTHESIS]:** MetroMart's unified return pipeline handles refunds identically across payment instruments.
- **Proposed Action [PROPOSED]:** Maintain current return operations; divert 100% of retention budget toward top-of-funnel checkout incentives.
- **Economic Potential:** ₹5,000,000 in saved engineering/optimization budget.

### 7. High Credit Limit Cardholder Defection Elasticity [MODEL_DERIVED]
- **Empirical Evidence:** Cardholders with limits $\ge$ ₹200,000 experienced an average spend defection of ₹8,420 vs ₹3,510 for limits < ₹200,000 (2.4x higher displacement).
- **Statistical Evidence:** Independent two-sample $t$-test: $t = 24.18, p < 0.0001$, Cohen's $d = 0.46$.
- **Hypothesis [HYPOTHESIS]:** Affluent cardholders require premium concierge and luxury travel milestone perks rather than transactional cashback.
- **Proposed Action [PROPOSED]:** Introduce VIP High-Limit Concierge Tier with 5% on Travel & Luxury + annual fee waivers.
- **Economic Potential:** ₹16,000,000 addressable recovery.
