# Business Problem & Context: MetroMart Inc. × HSIC Bank

## 1. Executive Context
MetroMart Inc. partnered with HSIC Bank to offer a premier co-branded credit card offering market-leading cashback propositions:
- **Prime Cardholders:** 5% Cashback on Grocery (Category 1), 3% Cashback on Electronics (Category 2), 1% on all other eligible purchases.
- **Non-Prime Cardholders:** 1% Cashback on all purchases.
- **Card Terms:** Zero annual/joining fees; monthly statement adjustment rewards.

## 2. The Core Strategic Dilemma: Silent Attrition
While MetroMart's overall sales expanded by +19.4% in FY26 (₹296.3M to ₹353.8M), HSIC Bank Credit Card Share of Wallet (SoW) collapsed:
- **FY25 SoW:** 28.91% (₹85.67M captured)
- **FY26 SoW:** 19.48% (₹68.92M captured)
- **Net SoW Contraction:** -9.43 percentage points (-32.6% relative loss of payment share).
- **Monthly Trajectory:** Peaked at 34.35% in Aug 2024 and tumbled to 14.65% in Jul 2026.

## 3. The Share-of-Wallet Definition & Governance Rules
$$\text{SoW} = \frac{\text{Net Spend on HSIC Card at MetroMart}}{\text{Total Net Spend by HSIC Cardholder at MetroMart}}$$
- Computed strictly over the period during which the customer held an active/open HSIC card.
- Calculated on **Net Sales** (Gross Sales minus Returns).
- Returns count as 0 positive transactions.
- MetroMart Fiscal Year runs from August 1 to July 31.
